# Creulette-tracker
A chrome extension that can be used to track your rolls in the web game Creulette!

# Features
1. Personal stats and roll count!
2. Leaderboards for your highest and lowest scoring rolls!
3. Best rolls in multiple categories, including brightness, saturation, beauty and more!

# How to use:
1. Download the `.zip` folder from this repository.
2. Unzip the file on your computer.
3. Paste `chrome://extensions` into your browser's address bar.
4. Toggle on `Developer mode` in the top right corner of your screen.
5. Click `Load unpacked` in the top left.
6. Find the folder you unzipped in the file explorer and select it.
7. The extension should be added to the top right bar of your browser. Now click it and open the link, or go to [the Creulette Website](https://prototypestudios.neocities.org/creulette) and open it there.
8. Your rolls will now automatically be tracked as long as the extension in toggled on. To see your progress, click the extension's icon or the puzzle piece in the top right and select `Creulette tracker`.
9. In the popup window, click the dropdown menu and select something to view your stats in different categories.
